The Problem

Cameras record. They don't respond.

Most CCTV systems are built to store footage, not to understand it — which means the moment that matters most can pass by unseen.

01

Passive by design

CCTV mostly records footage. It doesn't think, flag, or interpret what's happening on screen — someone has to go looking for it.

02

No one is always watching

Security teams can't monitor every feed, every second. Without help, incidents may not be noticed in real time.

03

Delay compounds damage

Every minute between an incident and a response increases potential damage and risk — for people and property alike.

The Solution

AlertEye turns footage into foresight.

AI-analyzed footage

AlertEye analyzes live CCTV footage continuously, using AI — not just after the fact, once it's too late to act.

Abnormal activity detection

Suspicious or abnormal activity patterns are detected as they unfold, not discovered later in a recorded review.

Real-time alerts

Security personnel get a real-time alert the moment something needs attention — where they already work.

"From footage to response — in seconds, not minutes."
